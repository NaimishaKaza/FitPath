## 🚀 How To Use FitPath:

### Step 1 — Create Your Account
- Visit https://fitpath-gentle-guide.lovable.app
- Click **Sign Up** and enter your email and password
- Check your inbox for a **verification email** and click the confirmation link
- You're in!

### Step 2 — Explore Your Dashboard
Once logged in, you'll land on your personal **Dashboard** — your home base. Here you can:
- See your fitness summary and progress at a glance
- Navigate to all features: AI Health Plans, Meal Scanner, Form Coach, and Vela
- Track your weekly goals and activity

### Step 3 — Generate Your AI Health Plan
- Go to **AI Health Plans**
- Enter your details: current weight, target weight, fitness goals, dietary preferences, and activity level
- FitPath's AI generates a **fully personalized weekly meal plan** just for you — covering both meals and workouts
- Your plan includes a day-by-day breakdown of what to eat and how to move

### Step 4 — Follow Your Meal Plan & Scan Your Food
- Your weekly **meal plan** is ready on your dashboard — breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks planned out
- Whenever you eat, head to the **AI Meal Scanner**
- Point your camera at your food and let the AI instantly identify your meal and log your calories automatically
- No manual calorie counting — just scan and go
- the user could also enter the calories manually by clicking on **Enter calories**

### Step 5 — Work Out With the AR Form Coach
- When it's time to exercise, open the **AI Form Coach** on your laptop
- Stand in front of your camera and start your workout
- The AR coach tracks your body movements in real time and gives you instant feedback on your posture and form
- Get corrections as you go — just like having a personal trainer at home

### Step 6 — Chat With Vela Anytime
- Have a question about your plan, your progress, or your health? Open **Vela**
- Vela is your 24/7 AI health agent — ask anything from "what should I eat tonight?" to "how do I fix my squat form?"
- Vela gives personalized, empathetic responses based on your goals

## 💡 What Problem Does FitPath Solve?

Getting fit is hard — not because people lack motivation, but because personalized guidance is 
expensive and inaccessible. Personal trainers cost hundreds of dollars a month. Nutritionists 
are out of reach for most people. Generic fitness apps give the same plan to everyone. And 
manually logging every meal is tedious enough that most people give up within a week.

FitPath eliminates all of these barriers. It brings together AI meal planning, real-time form 
correction, and a conversational health agent into one free, browser-based app — so anyone 
can access the quality of guidance that used to require a full team of professionals.

## 👤 Who Is FitPath For?

- **Beginners** who don't know where to start with fitness or nutrition
- **Busy individuals** who want a personalized plan without spending hours researching
- **People managing their weight** who need daily structure and accountability
- **Home workout enthusiasts** who want real-time form feedback without a gym trainer
- **Anyone tired of manually logging calories** who wants a faster, smarter way to track meals
